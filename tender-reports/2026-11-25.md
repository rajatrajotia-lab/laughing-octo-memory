## Usual eligibility rule of thumb (used once credentials arrive)
- **NHAI/MoRTH EPC:** one similar work ≥ ~50% of estimated cost (or two ≥ ~40%), avg annual turnover
  ≥ ~30–35%; JV of up to 3 members allowed, lead member usually ≥ 51% share.
- **Odisha Works/RD/WR:** registered class decides the cap (Special Class for big works); similar-work
  and turnover tests apply as in the bid document.
